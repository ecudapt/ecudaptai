MEMORY EFFICIENCY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Without Optimization:
  7B model training: ~28GB VRAM ❌ (impossible on consumer GPUs)

With QLoRA + LoRA:
  7B model training: ~8GB VRAM ✅ (fits on RTX 3080/3090)
  2.7B model training: ~4GB VRAM ✅ (fits on RTX 3060)

TRAINING SPEED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

On RTX 4090:
  - Mistral-7B: ~2-3 hours (3 epochs)
  - Phi-2: ~1 hour (5 epochs)

On RTX 3090:
  - Mistral-7B: ~4-6 hours (3 epochs)
  - Phi-2: ~2 hours (5 epochs)

On CPU (not recommended):
  - Phi-2: ~48 hours (5 epochs)
